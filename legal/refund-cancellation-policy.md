## 2. Order rejection or cancellation by the restaurant

If a restaurant rejects your order (e.g. closed, item unavailable, unable to fulfill) or
cancels it after acceptance (e.g. kitchen issue), you receive a **full refund**
automatically to your original payment method. No action is needed on your part.

## 3. Issues with a delivered/picked-up order

If your order arrives incorrect, incomplete, or of unacceptable quality, contact
+1 (661) 200-3055 (confirm dedicated support email before launch) (or use in-app support, once built) within 24 hours
of receiving it, with details (and photos, if applicable). Depending on the issue, we or
the restaurant may offer:

- A full or partial refund for the affected item(s), or
- A credit toward a future order, or
- A replacement item on a future order.

Refund decisions for quality/accuracy complaints are made case-by-case by restaurant
staff or platform support staff, based on the evidence provided.

## 4. How refunds are processed

- All refunds are issued by restaurant staff (or platform support, on their behalf)
  through Clover, back to your **original payment method**. We do not issue cash
  refunds or refunds to a different payment method.
- Refunds can be full or partial (e.g. refunding just one item's cost rather than the
  whole order).
- Processing time back to your card/bank is determined by Clover and your card issuer,
  typically 5-10 business days - not something we
  control directly.
- You'll see the refund reflected in your order history in-app once it's issued.

## 5. Tips

Tips are paid to the restaurant/delivery staff. If an order is fully refunded because it
was never fulfilled, the tip is refunded along with the rest of the order. For
partial-order issues, tip adjustments are handled case-by-case.
