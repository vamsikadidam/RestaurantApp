## 1. Delivery area

Each restaurant location sets its own delivery radius. At checkout, the app tells you
whether delivery is currently available to your address; addresses outside a
restaurant's configured delivery radius cannot be selected for delivery (pickup remains
available).

## 2. Delivery fees and minimum order

- A delivery fee (flat amount and/or a percentage-based service fee) may apply, shown
  before you confirm your order. Restaurants configure their own fee structure.
- Some restaurants set a minimum order subtotal for delivery; this is enforced at
  checkout and shown before you add items.

## 3. Estimated delivery time

Estimated delivery/prep times shown in-app are estimates based on the restaurant's
configured default prep time and are not guaranteed. Actual times can vary due to order
volume, weather, traffic, or restaurant-specific delays.

## 5. Delivery instructions and contact

You can add delivery instructions (e.g. gate code, building/unit number, "leave at
door") when placing an order. Provide accurate contact information - the restaurant or
delivery driver may need to reach you if there's an issue locating you.

## 6. Failed or delayed deliveries

- If a delivery cannot be completed because no one is available to receive it at the
  provided address, and it isn't safe/appropriate to leave unattended, contact
  +1 (661) 200-3055 (confirm dedicated support email before launch) as soon as possible. Refund eligibility for failed deliveries is
  covered by `refund-cancellation-policy.md`.
- If your order is significantly delayed beyond the estimated time, check your order
  status in-app first; contact +1 (661) 200-3055 (confirm dedicated support email before launch) if you have concerns.

## 7. Order accuracy at delivery

Please check your order upon receipt. Report any missing or incorrect items per
`refund-cancellation-policy.md` §3.
